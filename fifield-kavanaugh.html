The Fifield–Kavanaugh File

Lyndsey Fifield, who in 2026 publicly made allegations about Graham Platner, was publicly vocal during the 2018 Brett Kavanaugh confirmation fight. This page documents exactly what she said then — recovered verbatim from Wayback Machine captures of her posts — and what she has said in 2026, with a link to the evidence for every line.

What this page is, and is not

It is a fact ledger. Every quote below is verbatim from an archived page or a retrieved article, dated, linked, and graded: verbatim-archived means the actual archived post was retrieved and read; verbatim-quoted means quoted verbatim in a credible retrieved source. Nothing is reconstructed, paraphrased-as-quote, or included without evidence.

It draws no conclusion. A person's public statements in 2018 neither prove nor disprove anything about any 2026 allegation — hers or anyone's. Where others have drawn the comparison, that is documented and attributed to them; where she has responded, her words are here too. She has made no on-record statement directly addressing the comparison as of the last update; her adjacent responses to political-motive criticism are included in full for fairness.

Personal vs. professional. In 2018 Fifield ran the Heritage Foundation's social media. Institutional posts are not attributable to her personally and are excluded; every item below is from her personal account or a personal statement to press.
